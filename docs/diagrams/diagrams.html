checked against the share price

Deterministic, in Decimal, reproducible from stored inputs. No model touches the arithmetic.

US Treasury 10-yearrisk-free rate, dated

Cash-flow DCF

With growth
FCF = CFO + interest·(1−t) − capex − stock comp base = avg FCF margin × revenue g = revenue record (≤ 20%) 5 yrs, fade 5 yrs → 2.5%

Cost of capital

CAPM, industry beta
Ke = Rf + β × ERP Kd = (Rf + spread)(1 − t) WACC = E/V·Ke + D/V·Kd

Earnings power

No growth at all: a floor
EPV = avg operating margin × revenue × (1 − t) ÷ WACC

Fair value and range

Equity = enterprise value + cash − debt, per diluted share
fair value = mean(DCF, EPV) range = [min, max] of the methods sensitivity = WACC ±1 pt × growth ±4 pts what-if = 6 editable assumptions, nothing saved
