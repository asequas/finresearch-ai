FinResearch AI · intrinsic value

Fair value from reported numbers, checked against the price

Deterministic, in Decimal, reproducible from stored inputs. No model touches the arithmetic.
Inputs, each stored with its source
SEC XBRL, 5 fiscal yearscash from operations, capex, stock comp, interest, revenue, operating income, cash, debt, diluted shares
US Treasury 10-yearrisk-free rate, dated
Damodaran industry dataunlevered beta, debt ratio, equity risk premium, default spreads
Cost of capital

CAPM WACC

Industry beta relevered at the industry debt ratio
Ke = Rf + β × ERP
Kd = (Rf + spread)(1 − t)
WACC = E/V·Ke + D/V·Kd
Two methods

Cash-flow DCF GROWTH

FCF = CFO + interest·(1−t) − capex − stock comp
base = 3-yr avg FCF margin × latest revenue
g = revenue record, capped at 20%, for 5 yrs
→ fades over 5 yrs → 2.5% forever

Earnings power value NO GROWTH

EPV = avg operating margin × revenue
× (1 − t) ÷ WACC
Result

Fair value and range

Midpoint of the methods; the range is where they land. Equity = EV + cash − debt, per diluted share.
